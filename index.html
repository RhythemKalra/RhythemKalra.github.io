track 02 · initiatives

Initiatives: where I do the work.

Designare COO

design community startup · 150K+ followers

A design community with a following of more than 150,000 people. As COO, my work is about turning ideas into organised activity: coordinating people and follow-ups, supporting programmes and events, talking to the community, and moving initiatives from planning to delivery.

  • Operations: keeping people, follow-ups and timelines moving
  • Sales & growth: sales planning and helping initiatives grow
  • Community, events & workshops: running activity people actually show up for
  • Partnerships: working with the people and brands around the community
  • Content: reel ideas, scripts, copy and community announcements
  • Product: product thinking and product management on Dazzle, a product we're building at Designare

Buzzinga OPERATIONS LEAD

social media agency · client work

As Operations Lead at Buzzinga, I take content from a brief to a plan the team can actually execute, for a lot of very different kinds of brands. It taught me how to connect creative thinking with timelines, client communication and the practical work of getting content made.

  • Content strategy: planning content around campaigns, launches and seasonal moments
  • Scripting & copy: ideas, scripts and copy for reels, carousels and static posts
  • Shooting: shooting content for brands
  • Client coordination: meeting actionables, next steps, feedback and approvals
  • Teamwork: coordinating between creative and account teams to get work into production
